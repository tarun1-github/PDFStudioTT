# Smart Sequence Detection User Guide

Available in **PDF Compiler** and **Learning Book Creator**:
1. Upload your images, PDFs, or Office/text documents in any order.
2. Review the **Smart Sequence Detection** table:
   - Check `Proposed order`, `Detected chapter`, `Detected page`, `Extracted heading/title`, `Detection method`, `Confidence`, and `Status`.
   - Inspect any **Duplicate page** or **Missing sequence number** warnings.
3. Choose a sorting mode (`Automatic Detection`, `Original Upload Order`, `Sort by Page Number`, `Sort by Chapter Number`, `Chapter then Page Number`, or `Manual Ordering`).
4. Edit `Proposed order`, `Detected chapter`, or `Extracted heading/title` directly in the table if needed, or click **Restore original order** / **Re-analyse**.
5. Click **Compile PDF** or **Build Learning Book PDF** to generate, preview, and download your ordered PDF.
