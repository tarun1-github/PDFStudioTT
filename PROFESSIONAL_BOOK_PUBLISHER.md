# Professional Learning Book Publisher

PDF Studio's **Learning Book Creator** provides a 10-stage publishing workflow:
1. **Cover Designer**: 4 templates (`Corporate Technical Manual`, `Cisco Learning Guide`, `Training Workbook`, `Minimal Professional`), Portrait/Landscape A4, optional logo, classification, version, and live PNG preview.
2. **Upload & Smart Sequence**: Integrates Phase 14 OCR/content sequencing.
3. **Chapters & Sections**: Hierarchical level 1 (Chapter) and level 2 (Section) arrangement.
4. **Clickable TOC & Nested Bookmarks**: Accurate final page numbering with clickable PDF links and deduplicated outline hierarchy.
5. **Headers, Footers & Page Numbering**: Cover unnumbered, preliminary TOC in Roman numerals (`i, ii, iii`), main chapters starting at `1` (`1, 2, 3` or `Page 1 of N`), configurable alignment.
6. **Chapter Dividers**: Optional `Classic Banner` or `Minimal Left Accent` divider pages.
7. **PDF Optimization & Metadata**: Editable PDF metadata and 4 compression modes.
