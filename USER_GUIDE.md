# PDF Studio User Guide

## Navigation
1. **Dashboard**: Live KPIs, job status distribution, and recent activity.
2. **Image to PDF**: Upload PNG/JPG/JPEG/BMP/TIFF/WEBP, reorder thumbnails, choose A4/A3/Letter/Original, portrait/landscape/auto, and margins.
3. **Document to PDF**: Convert DOCX, TXT, MD, HTML, XLSX, and PPTX to verified PDFs.
4. **PDF Compiler**: Combine mixed images, documents, and PDFs in your exact order with custom section bookmarks.
5. **Learning Book Creator**: Create structured manuals with cover page, Table of Contents, 3 templates (`Corporate Technical Manual`, `AI Learning Notes`, `Sketchnote Collection`), headers/footers, page numbers, and PDF bookmarks.
6. **PDF Manager**: Merge, split, extract pages, rotate, compress, number pages, add headers/footers, watermark, bookmark, and edit metadata.
7. **Shared Library**: Download approved team documents; Team Leads and Admins can publish documents to shared categories.
8. **Processing History**: View job history and download personal generated PDFs.
9. **User Management** *(Admin)*: Manage team users, roles, and view audit logs.
10. **Settings**: Inspect database/dependency health and update application settings.
